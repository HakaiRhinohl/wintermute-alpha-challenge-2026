Case 08 · First Move

Safe means published before the retained L1 head

The safe head is a decision boundary. It rules out L2 data that was not yet made visible on Ethereum at the game’s retained L1 head.

1. Freeze the L1 context

The game retains one Ethereum block hash. Nothing published after it may influence the first move.

retained L1 head
25,785,478
→test each L2 batch against this fixed point

2. Ask the right question

For an L2 block, inspect when its batch became included on L1.

Candidate AL1 origin: 25,785,450
batch included: 25,785,468
Candidate BL1 origin: 25,785,450
batch included: 25,785,492
L1 origin alone is insufficient. Both candidates can derive from an old L1 block, while only one was published in time.
→classify by batch inclusion, not origin alone
Candidate A: SAFE25,785,468 ≤ retained head 25,785,478Its L2 state may support the honest claim.
Candidate B: UNSAFE25,785,492 > retained head 25,785,478It may exist today, but was not available at game time.
honest L2 block = min(theoretical split boundary, L2 safe head)
